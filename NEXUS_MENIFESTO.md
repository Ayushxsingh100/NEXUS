1. PROJECT VISION

Project Nexus is not a portfolio.

It is an interactive cinematic experience that visualizes the journey of an engineer's mind.

Visitors should not feel like they are browsing a website.

They should feel like they are experiencing a carefully directed story where every chapter reveals another layer of curiosity, engineering, growth, and creation.

The objective is to leave visitors remembering the experience rather than simply the projects.

2. CORE PHILOSOPHY

Everything in Project Nexus is built around one belief:

Every engineer begins with curiosity. Every great engineer never stops questioning.

The project is not about space.

It is not about graphics.

It is not about technology.

Technology is only the medium.

The real story is about:

Curiosity
Thought
Learning
Engineering
Creation
Legacy
3. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every design decision must satisfy these principles.

Principle 1

Story comes before visuals.

Principle 2

Emotion comes before animation.

Principle 3

Typography is the main character.

Principle 4

Motion should feel intentional.

Nothing moves without purpose.

Principle 5

Silence is part of the design.

Not every moment needs movement.

Principle 6

Minimalism creates impact.

Remove everything that does not contribute to the story.

7. MOTION LANGUAGE

Motion should feel:

Slow
Confident
Deliberate
Cinematic

Avoid:

Fast movement
Flashy transitions
Over-animation
Sudden camera changes
8. CAMERA LANGUAGE

The camera represents the visitor.

Rules:

Slow forward movement
Gentle floating
No aggressive rotations
No shaking
No dramatic zooms

10. THREE.JS PHILOSOPHY

Three.js is no longer used for spectacle.

It is used as a cinematic stage.

Responsibilities:

Camera
Lighting
Depth
Fog
Typography
Glass surfaces
Minimal 3D environments

Avoid:

Large particle systems
Heavy shaders
GPU simulations
Complex procedural geometry
11. PERFORMANCE RULES

Target:

60 FPS

Designed for mid-range laptops.

Rules:

Minimal draw calls
Shared materials
Shared geometries
No per-frame allocations
Minimal shaders
Simplicity over complexity

17. SUCCESS CRITERIA

When someone finishes Project Nexus, they should think:

"That was unlike any portfolio I've seen."

Not:

"That had cool animations."

18. THE GOLDEN RULE

Every feature must answer YES to both questions:

Does this make the story stronger?
Can this be achieved in a simpler way?

If either answer is NO, the feature should not be built.

19. THE PROJECT MANTRA

This sentence guides every future decision:

Project Nexus is not a journey through space. It is a journey through the architecture of an engineer's mind.
